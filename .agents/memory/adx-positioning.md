name: Arrington Digital positioning
description: User-approved geographic scope and hero direction for the Arrington Digital website.
---

The main Arrington Digital site should represent both the U.S. and Jamaica and the range of industries ADx serves. Jamaica-specific services, locations, and context belong on a dedicated Arrington Digital Limited landing page.

**Why:** the user clarified this after earlier concepts made Jamaica the parent brand's lead story.

**How to apply:** keep global pages and industry/service pages relevant to both markets; place island-specific details on the Jamaica Limited page.

The homepage hero should use a bold orange brand field, with “Build,” “Market,” and “Scale” all in the same color. Use “ADX” consistently in customer-facing copy. Video captions should name the work or industry, not a location; include the restaurant apron scene and the team scene may be labeled “ADX in motion.”

**Why:** the user wants a more attention-grabbing, industry-led first impression while preserving the existing logo, tagline, navigation, and palette; they later clarified the three tagline verbs should match and the brand abbreviation should be uppercase.

**How to apply:** carry these preferences into future ADX homepage and hero revisions.

Keep employee quotes and portraits from the live site. Do not display pricing or media-source credits. Use distinct images across landing pages, except for user-supplied product imagery. Exclude agriculture, car rental, and mobility; combine resorts, hotels, villas, and real estate as one category, with a numbered label and short blurb for each industry. Order the What We Do menu with What We Do first, then ADX Surge, Vicki Ann, and ADX LaunchIQ; order Company with About, U.S., and Jamaica first.

**Why:** the user clarified which proof, imagery, industries, and navigation structure should be preserved or excluded.

**How to apply:** keep these content and navigation rules consistent across the Arrington Digital homepage, menus, and landing pages.

Do not create a standalone “Who We Help” umbrella page or a separate “Start a Conversation” landing page; keep industry pages accessible from the menu and use Contact for inquiries. Base Careers and legal pages on the live-site content. Use relevant HD or 4K royalty-free photography on each landing page without visible source credits.

**Why:** the user asked for fewer unnecessary landing pages, continuity with current Careers and legal content, and stronger image quality.

**How to apply:** use Contact as the inquiry destination, preserve useful audience-specific routes, and choose relevant high-resolution licensed imagery for new landing pages.
